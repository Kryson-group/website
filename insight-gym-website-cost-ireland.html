How Much Does a Gym Website
Cost in Ireland? (2025 Guide)

5 min read • Web Design • Ireland

If you run a gym or fitness centre in Ireland and you're thinking about getting a website, the first question is usually the same: how much is this going to cost? The honest answer depends on what you actually need — and what you're willing to sacrifice.

DIY Website Builders (Wix, Squarespace): €0–€30/month

Tools like Wix and Squarespace let you build a site yourself using drag-and-drop templates. On the surface they look affordable. In practice, the problems compound quickly.

Template designs are used by thousands of other businesses. Your gym looks like every other gym. Load times are slow because the platforms are bloated. SEO is limited — you can't add proper schema markup, you're restricted to basic meta tags, and your rankings suffer. There's also an ongoing monthly fee that adds up to €360+ per year, forever, for something you don't own.

For a gym with a serious reputation, a Wix site undermines trust before a prospective member has even read a word.

Freelance Developers: €500–€3,000

Hiring a freelance developer gives you more flexibility but comes with risk. Quality varies enormously. Timelines slip. Communication breaks down. And you rarely get the local SEO knowledge that's critical for a gym trying to rank in their area.

Agencies (General): €3,000–€10,000+

A general web agency will build you something, but they're not specialists in health and wellness. You'll spend time educating them about your industry, your members, and what your customers actually search for. The price reflects their overheads, not necessarily better results for you.

What Should a Gym Website Actually Include?

Regardless of how you build it, a gym website in Ireland needs at minimum:

  • A clear homepage with your location, class types, and pricing
  • Mobile-responsive design (over 70% of searches are on mobile)
  • A contact form or online booking system
  • Google Business Profile integration
  • Local SEO targeting your town/city (e.g. "gym in Galway", "fitness centre Dublin")
  • Fast load times — under 3 seconds on mobile

The Real Cost of a Bad Website

A gym website that doesn't rank in local search, doesn't load on mobile, or looks outdated is actively costing you members every month. If even one new member signs up as a result of a professional website — at €50–€80/month — it pays for itself within a year. Most gyms see a return on their website investment within the first 2–3 months.
