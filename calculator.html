How these numbers are calculated: Day 30 assumes 25% recovery of speed-to-lead and show-rate leaks as new processes are put in place. Day 60 adds 40% recovery of booking-rate and close-rate gaps as the team adapts. Day 90 adds 60% of the follow-up leak once the sequence is running. No business recovers 100% of leaks in 90 days. These are deliberately conservative figures that reflect a realistic embedding period. Full results typically take 3–6 months depending on team size and existing infrastructure.
