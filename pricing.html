Frequently Asked Questions

Do I own the website once it's built? +
Yes, 100%. Once the project is complete and paid, you own everything outright. We hand over all code, hosting credentials, and domain access. No lock-in, no ongoing fees to Kryson.
What are the ongoing costs after launch? +
Our build fee is a one-time payment. The only ongoing costs are hosting (typically €5–€15/month, paid directly to the host) and your domain name (around €10–€15/year). We don't add any markup to these. If you have the Growth or Complete package, Stripe charges a small transaction fee (currently 1.5% + 25c for European cards) directly to Stripe.
What do you need from me to get started? +
Your logo, photos of your space (or we'll advise on getting them), your pricing and services, and a 30-minute call to understand your business. We handle all copy, design, and technical setup.
Can I upgrade my package later? +
Yes. Many clients start on Starter or Growth and upgrade when they're ready. We offer upgrade pricing so you only pay the difference, not the full new package price.
What's included in local SEO? +
We set up and optimise your Google Business Profile, add structured data (JSON-LD schema) to your site, optimise page titles and meta descriptions for local search terms (e.g. "yoga studio Dublin", "gym near Galway"), build citations on 20+ Irish directories, and submit your sitemap to Google Search Console. For the Growth package, we also work on Google Maps ranking specifically.
How does the Stripe integration work? +
We connect your Stripe account directly to your website. Customers can pay for classes, memberships, and packages online. You see all payments in your Stripe dashboard in real time. Stripe charges go directly to your bank account — we never touch your funds.
Do you build for businesses outside of Ireland? +
Our focus is the Irish market but we take UK and international projects on a case-by-case basis. Get in touch and we'll let you know.
